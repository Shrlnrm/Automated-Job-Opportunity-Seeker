Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AJOS ("we", "our", "the platform") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA Malaysia). This policy explains what data we collect, how it is handled, and your privacy rights.

1. What We Collect

  • Account & Authentication: Email address, login method (email/password or Google OAuth), and account timestamps managed via Firebase Authentication.
  • User Profile & Resume Information: Optional profile fields you provide (such as your full name, education history, technical skills, project links, portfolio/LinkedIn URLs, and uploaded resume text) used exclusively to personalize job search matching and generate cover letter drafts.
  • Temporary Search Context: Your recent job search query, filters, and company lookups are stored temporarily (up to 2 hours) to restore your active work session across tab switches. Expired session caches are automatically deleted.
  • Account Quota Metadata: Monthly search limits and rolling reset dates to enforce fair usage.

2. Data Privacy & Zero Monetization

  • We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information, resume content, or search queries with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or recruitment agencies.
  • We do not collect or store financial or payment card data.
  • We do not retain permanent search histories or tracking profiles.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to provide platform features:

  • Authenticate and protect your account session securely.
  • Enforce your monthly quota limits.
  • Extract resume details and generate AI-assisted cover letter and outreach suggestions at your explicit request.
  • Restore your immediate workspace state upon re-login.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account profile data is retained as long as your account remains active. Temporary search caches expire within 2 hours. You have the right to request immediate and complete account and data erasure at any time.

5. Third-Party Sub-processors

We work with vetted infrastructure and API providers to power core features:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Authentication, session management, and encrypted Firestore database hosting.
  • Cloudflare: Turnstile bot detection and edge network security.
  • Google Places & Serper APIs: Real-time job posting aggregation and company directory data.
  • AI Model Services: Natural language processing for resume parsing and cover letter draft generation.

6. Your Rights (PDPA Malaysia & Privacy Rights)

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Review: View and edit your profile and resume data directly in the Profile dashboard.
  • Correction: Update your name, skills, links, or password at any time.
  • Erasure: Permanently delete your account and all associated profile records by contacting platform administration.

7. Security Controls

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Database access is strictly governed by server-side Firebase Admin SDK rules, token validation, rate limiters, and anti-bot challenges.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, data export, or account deletion requests, please contact the platform administrator directly.
